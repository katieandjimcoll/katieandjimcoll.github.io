Our Story

From Eclipse to Happily Ever After

Our love story began on a crowded hotel rooftop during a solar eclipse.

In August 2017, during the first week of graduate classes at the University of Kansas, Katie was rushing across campus desperately trying to find a classroom she’d never been to before. When she finally burst through the door, she found an empty room and a last-minute email from her professor: class was postponed so everyone could witness the historic solar eclipse.

Right at that moment, a message popped up in the graduate student group chat. It was Jim, inviting anyone and everyone to join him on the rooftop of the Oread Hotel to watch the sky. Since the hotel was only a block away, Katie figured she had nothing to lose. Plus, she thought, this Jim guy is kind of cute.

When Katie arrived, the rooftop was packed. But tucked against a wall, saving just enough space for two, was Jim. She made her way over to him, completely unaware she was taking her first steps toward her future husband. While the rooftop eventually ran out of eclipse glasses, neither of them minded. They spent the rest of the afternoon talking, laughing, and looking at each other instead of the sky.

Over the next few years in Lawrence, Kansas, we fell completely in love. Our days were filled with exploring sunflower fields, visiting ice castles, and returning to that same rooftop bar. Along the way, we grew our little family; The first a “clear the shelter day” cat, Lucy and later, when Jim surprised Katie with an only loosly discussed Christmas gift: our dog, Moose.

In 2021, Jim accepted a position at the National Water Center in Tuscaloosa, Alabama. Ready for a new adventure, we packed up our lives and headed south. Jim says this was the moment he knew we were “all-in”. After all, if Katie was willing to move to Alabama for him, it had to be the real deal. A year later, another career opportunity brought us to Fort Collins, Colorado, and Jim knew it was finally time to ask Katie to be his wife.

The proposal was also a bit of a surprise. While Katie was visiting family home in Aberdeen, South Dakota, Jim secretly drove through the night. The next morning (Katie’s birthday), she was thoroughly confused as to why her dad was practically forcing her out of the house. She walked into the backyard to find Jim, fast asleep in the hammock, holding a bunch of balloons.

The next day, after taking Moose for a walk to a local coffee shop, Jim got down on one knee in the backyard of Katie’s childhood home. After shaking off the absolute shock, Katie said, “Yes!”

Now, after a few wonderful years of being engaged, we are finally making it official, surrounded by the people we love most. We can’t wait to share a wedding weekend that reflects everything we love to do together: brunch, yard games, endless laughter, and quality time with you. See you there!
