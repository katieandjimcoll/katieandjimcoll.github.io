Q&A

Common Questions and Logistics

Where are the ceremony and reception being held?

Both events will take place within Wylie Park in Aberdeen, South Dakota, but at two different locations within the park.

The ceremony will be held at the Castle in Storybook Land…

Storybook Land (Ceremony) 2300 24th Ave NW, Aberdeen, SD 57401

… followed by the reception at the Wylie Park Pavilion overlooking Wylie Lake.

Wylie Park Pavillion (Reception) 2306 24th Ave NW, Aberdeen, SD 57401

What are the venues like?

The Castle is one of the first landmarks you’ll see upon entering Storybook Land. Our ceremony will take place in the Castle courtyard, an outdoor setting with sun shades for guest comfort. The reception will be held in and around the Wylie Park Pavilion, a spacious rustic venue that is not air-conditioned. The surrounding lawn area will be used for yard games and offers beautiful views of Wylie Lake. Please note that Wylie Park is a popular destination for families and travelers, especially on holiday weekends. We kindly ask our guests to be respectful of other park visitors throughout the day.

What should I wear?

Dressy-casual attire is encouraged. Comfortable shoes are recommended, as there will be some walking between venues and portions of the celebration will take place on grass. While the wedding party will be dressed in semi-formal attire, guests are welcome to dress up or keep things more casual—whatever makes you feel most comfortable.

What happens if there is bad weather?

If rain is expected during the ceremony, the ceremony will be moved to the Wylie Park Pavilion, where the reception will also be held. The Pavilion is large enough to accommodate both the ceremony and reception activities, including some of the planned yard games if outdoor spaces become unusable. If temperatures are unusually high, we’re hopeful that our brunch celebration will help us avoid the hottest part of the day. Because the Pavilion is not air-conditioned, we completely understand if anyone needs to step away or leave early to prioritize their comfort and well-being.

How do I RSVP? When is the deadline?

You can RSVP here,or visit the homepage of our wedding website and click the RSVP button. Enter the names listed on your invitation, then select Accept or Decline. If you plan to attend, please include any dietary restrictions or accommodations (gluten-free, vegan, allergies, etc.) so we can ensure everyone enjoys the meal.

Please RSVP by August 3, 2026.

Do you have a registry?

Your presence at our wedding is truly the greatest gift we could receive.

For those who would like to celebrate with a gift, we have put together a collection of items and experiences that will help us build our life together. Rather than creating a registry with a specific store, we have curated a list of gift ideas from a variety of stores and websites, which can be found here or under the Registry tab on our wedding website. Feel free to use these links, or purchase similar items elsewhere. We would also be grateful for contributions toward our honeymoon and future home fund. For guests who prefer to give electronically, contributions can be made via Venmo.
