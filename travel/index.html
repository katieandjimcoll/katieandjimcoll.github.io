Travel & Accommodations

✈️ Getting to Aberdeen

If you are flying, Aberdeen Regional Airport (ABR) offers daily Delta Air Lines connections through Minneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport.

For additional airline options, nearby airports include:

  • Watertown Regional Airport (ATY) (~1 hour 30 minute drive) — Offers Delta and United connections through Minneapolis and Denver.
  • Fargo, North Dakota (FAR) (~3-hour drive)
  • Sioux Falls, South Dakota (FSD) (~3-hour drive)
  • Minneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport (MSP) (~5-hour drive)
